Additional questions to consider while answering. They are questions, not requirements, and not
criteria to check the system against. They do not say what the answers should be.

- The accepted intent says more than one delivery provider is expected over time. Concretely,
  what differs between one provider and another in this system?
- Which parts of the system currently hold that knowledge?
- After this change, which parts hold it that did not hold it before?
- The accepted intent says the outcome vocabulary is part of the product contract and does not
  change. Which parts of the system depend on that stability, and what did this change require
  of them?
